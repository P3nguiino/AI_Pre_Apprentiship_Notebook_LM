# Meet [LucAIs]

## 1. Project Overview
My AI Career Coach is designed to help me (William Duran)
with attaining skills in the cybersecurity field to land a job after graduation.

**Career pathway:** Cybersecurity

**Built with:** Google NotebookLM

## 2. The Problem I'm Solving
I am trying to help people like myself be able to land jobs in the cybersecurity field out of college because in my time around the tech field cybersecurity is always seen as a mid level or higher job field. I want to make it possible for students out of college be able to develop the neccessary skills, knowledge, and experience to land a job in this field out of college.

Why would someone benefit from this tool?
They would benefit by being able to get guidance of how to break in the cybersecurity field straight out of high school.

## 5. Testing and Improving My Assistant
**Original prompt:** What are the best internships I can get?

**Improved prompt:** What are the best internships I can get for someone who is in their Junior year of their CS degree and has had 3 IT/Tech roles internships before and only a A+ certificate and who goes to Cal State LA.

**What changed and why:**
The second prompt helped my assistant in being able to depict what specific roles I should be looking at in my area instead of just giving me a broad list of jobs. It also adjusted the level and companies I should be looking at according to what I already have done.

## 6. Responsible AI
How did I check the AI's output for accuracy,
bias, or misleading information?
I made sure to double check it with external sources of the same topic and also checked the credibility of the 3 main sources it is using.

What information should never be shared
with this assistant?
Any personal information about any human liek SSNs, phone numbers, emails, passwords, addresses. Also very high detailed code or information about companies or softwares.
